# Automotive Sales & Showroom Analytics — Power BI

A multi-page dashboard built with **automotive showroom training data**. This is a learning project, not a report of a real dealership's performance.

## Business questions
- How are sales performing across vehicle brands and models?
- What share of cars has sold, and what value remains in unsold inventory?
- What do customer and maintenance views add to the sales story?

## Work shown
The report contains an executive overview and pages for sales, inventory, service, customer insights, and a vehicle studio for exploring brands and models. It uses Power Query for cleaning, a data model and DAX measures for analysis, and slicers, drilldown, drillthrough, tooltips, bookmarks, and page navigation for exploration.

The reported metrics include average sale price, sold car percentage, and unsold inventory value.
