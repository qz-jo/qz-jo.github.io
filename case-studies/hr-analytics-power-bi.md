# HR Headcount & Attrition — Power BI

A learning dashboard that presents workforce metrics and lets viewers explore HR data by department, location, and employment status.

## Business questions
- What are the current headcount, terminations, and attrition rate?
- How do salary and age vary across the workforce?
- Where do department, gender, and monthly hiring patterns stand?

## Work shown
The report includes headcount, terminations, attrition rate, average salary and age, department and gender breakdowns, monthly hires, and employee detail. Saif describes using Power Query for cleaning, data modeling, DAX measures, and filters for Department, Location, and Employment Status.
